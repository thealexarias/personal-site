Observability

I've enjoyed using goatcounter for this websites observability but I crave more! I began setting up metrics for an app I manage at work and I love this field of software. I figured I'd apply what I'm learning to my personal life. I've yet to implement anything on this site but Prometheus + Grafana will be my weapons of choice. My hope is to better understand who or what demographic I'm appealing and use that data to guide my decisions on what to spend my time working on. I have many interests in life and find it hard to focus on one or a few of them but if I discover other's are interested in a particular thing I'm doing then I could focus on that more knowing there is an audience for it.

Exercise

Running. Currently I'm obsessed with running, the community, the data, improving, the fashion, the health benefits. Everything about it. I'm definitely being influenced by this instagram account and I'm totally ok with that. I've doubled my milage since the obsession took a hold of me. From 9 to 18+ miles per week.

Abandoning hledger

I tried to adopt hledger as a new more complex financial tracking system but it has been 4+ months and I have yet to adopt it and remain tracking with pen and paper. I figured as my income increases then the way I track my finances should change but I guess I'm a sucker for simplicity or I'm stubborn and refuse to change my ways. Either way I will remain with analog and will attempt to digitize my tracking system instead. With AI by my side I'm excited to build a custom solution that should feel identical to my current setup
